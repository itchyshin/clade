# Policy- and age-specific demographic replay

Replay the complete previous density-by-threshold-by-food-by-founder-age experiment, using its 100 archived source panels and all 14,400 mixture and monoculture simulations. An isolated Julia research hook observes each grass bite during agent update. Record births by parent policy and age, deaths by individual policy and age, grass intake by consumer policy and age, and pre-mortality exposure in agent-ticks in age bands 0–49, 50–99, 100–149 and 150+. Track inheritance by exact haploid policy mean with mutation disabled. An individual's age is its age after that tick's increment. Newborns enter exposure at the next tick.

At every tick, check the intake total against grass depletion and births and deaths against the kernel counters and population identity. At every 50-tick checkpoint compare population, births, deaths, intake, exposure and initial physical hash with the previous run. Extinction is padded through tick 1,000 without dropping any accumulated events. The observer must not alter the simulation RNG or trajectories. Pilot one existing block across all 144 cells, check all 2,880 windows and policy-age conservation, and estimate runtime before the full replay. If any identity, conservation or replay check fails, diagnose it before proceeding.

For each seed, factorial cell and 1–1,000 window, calculate the mixture-minus-average-eight-controls difference in births, deaths and grass intake. Limited minus original food gives the food effect on each difference. Changes in that food effect when threshold is lowered (120 to 80) and density raised (48 to 96) are paired across seeds and reported by age and policy as exploratory descriptive decompositions. Initial population differences are zero within a mixture/control cell, so food effects on population contrast equal those on births minus deaths; verify this identity before summarizing. Food intake and demographic differences are downstream outcomes, not independently randomized mediators. Total intake combines population exposure and intake per agent; retain age-policy agent-ticks and report exposure-normalized rates separately where denominators permit. Policy-specific mixture contributions are compared with one eighth of the matched monoculture so they sum to the overall mixture-minus-average-control contrast; do not interpret lineage or age decompositions as independent causal effects.

The endpoints, age bands and 1,000-tick period were chosen after observing the prior factorial. All subgroup and period comparisons are exploratory and unadjusted for multiplicity. Retain all runs including extinction, bottlenecks and weak endpoints. Results pertain to the source-panel, clonal haploid world and the combined initial-grass/regrowth treatments; the observer measures grass only, not additional resource layers. Archive the research-only patch, observer source, raw per-window records, checksums and receipts.
