# Stable-world reproductive timing study

## Question and setting

Does restricting young-parent reproductive opportunities delay the first subsequent birth, and does it change the population difference between a policy mixture and balanced single-policy controls? The previous dose pilot tracked only parents who had been blocked, omitted sham parents from that follow-up and used a setting with frequent bottlenecks. This study enters each individual at their first energy-, age- and cap-eligible opportunity while younger than 50, irrespective of that opportunity's gate decision, including sham. Track birth, death, energy and end of observation. Births on the entry tick have elapsed time zero. Because population histories diverge, the individuals entering a cohort under different doses are post-intervention populations; dose-specific timing contrasts are descriptive intervention outcomes, not individual-level counterfactual postponement or natural mediation estimates.

Use the previously validated lower-bottleneck setting: 96 assay founders and 100 source founders, reproductive threshold 80, cap 700 on a 900-cell grid, unique initial positions, haploid source policy panels and no assay mutation. Cross five nested young-gate doses (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) with original versus limited food and same-age versus staggered founders, with one mixture and eight equally weighted monocultures: 180 assays per source block. Initial physical state and policy panel must match across all doses and policies within each food/age world. Source dynamics use mutation .2 and are never gated. The food treatments change both initial grass and regrowth. The gate is applied before space, mate and reproductive-cost checks, and preserves potential cost on blocked attempts.

## Estimands and checks

The primary timing estimand is the Aalen-Johansen cumulative incidence of a parent's first birth within 50 ticks of cohort entry, treating death before birth as a competing event and end of simulation as administrative right censoring. Estimate separately for each run, with a full 50-tick-entry-window sensitivity excluding entrants after tick 950. A seed-level equally weighted mixture-minus-mean-of-eight-controls contrast and a dose-minus-sham contrast preserve source blocks as independent units. Summarize the original food / same-age mixture at all doses as a descriptive dose response; report differences with paired seed-level 95% intervals, without treating individual agents as independent replicate seeds. Report competing death and censoring counts, cohort sizes, energy at entry and birth, and the risk set at 50. Zero-time births and ties follow the simulation ordering: death is processed before the reproduction gate, so entry-tick deaths are impossible and births take precedence only if actually recorded before death. No eligible parents or source failures are reported rather than replaced.

The primary population estimand is the endpoint mixture-minus-mean-of-eight-controls contrast at each dose for each food and age world, and its dose-minus-sham difference; food contrasts are secondary. Estimate seed means and 95% paired t intervals and inspect seed distributions. All planned population runs remain included irrespective of viability. Effect modification and multiple doses are exploratory; no claim of equivalence from non-significance.

Run a five-source pilot on 82001–82005 plus prior-source sham replay 51001. Require all source endpoints viable, at least 95% of assay endpoints viable, at least 95% of assay histories never below 20, fewer than 1% of source and assay tick observations at the 700 cap, exact same-start matching, eight distinct source policies with behavioural disagreement, event-to-cohort reconciliation and sham replay of the prior stable-world run metrics. The 20-agent threshold is operational, not proof of genetic or ecological stability. If the pilot fails, report and revise the design without discarding results. If it passes, run 100 independent source blocks 83001–83100, retaining all planned outcomes and reporting the same diagnostics without a post hoc stability filter.

Use an independent Python reconstruction of gate decisions and individual cohort records from all pilot event rows, plus small hand-calculated competing-risk and censoring fixtures checked against R survival. Track full individual cohorts, run-level opportunity and birth totals, population extrema and source states. Audit final counts, matching, source hashes and cohort consistency; store full cohorts and events on Totoro with checksums. Source-level uncertainty is based on 100 independent blocks, not the much larger agent count.

## Compute

Run on Totoro with Julia threads four and BLAS one, at most 24 concurrent workers (96 cores). The five-seed pilot plus replay is estimated at 3–6 minutes from the previous 900-assay 116-second pilot, with overhead for larger populations and event checks. The 100-seed campaign is estimated at 20–45 minutes; enforce a 45-minute deadline and re-report if exceeded. The independent analysis and archive checks follow the run.
