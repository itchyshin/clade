# Stable population screen and independent food replication

The previous endpoint-viable populations often fell below 20 individuals. Define a bottleneck here as fewer than 20 live agents at any tick, in either source training or the assay. This is an operational threshold, not proof of biological or genetic stability. All simulation outcomes remain in the analysis; never filter or replace seeds to obtain stability.

Screen three candidate settings in this order: 96 assay founders and 100 source founders, with reproductive energy threshold 120, 100, or 80. The previous settings had 48 and 50 founders respectively and threshold 120. Both food treatments, the fixed and staggered founder-age schedules, eight policies and eight single-policy controls remain as in the preceding experiment. Staggered ages 25–72 are each represented twice among the 96 founders. Source evolution has mutation SD .20; the assay has mutation disabled. The increased starting density and any change to the reproductive threshold are part of the new world and prevent attribution to stability alone.

Use pilot seeds 50001–50005 for each candidate. Choose the first candidate in the stated order with all five source histories at or above 20, at least 95% of all 180 assay histories at or above 20, at least 90% of seed blocks with all 36 assay runs above the threshold, all source endpoints having at least eight distinct behaviourally differing policies, and less than 1% of assay tick observations at the 500-agent hard cap. Also require exact population accounting and initial-state matching. Screen only those stability and validity metrics, not the magnitude or direction of the mixture contrast. Pilot blocks are not included in the main results.

If no candidate passes, inspect the failure reasons and report them before selecting a revised design; do not present unstable results as the requested stable-world answer. If one passes, run the first passing candidate on independent seeds 51001–51100, 36 assay simulations and one source simulation per block. Report the mixture-minus-mean-of-eight-controls difference at tick 800 and its change to tick 1,000, separately for each food and age condition, with 95% paired-seed intervals and bootstrap sensitivity. Food and age contrasts are secondary, and all intervals are unadjusted for multiplicity. Retain all outcomes and failed source blocks; report actual bottleneck and cap rates. A successful pilot does not guarantee stability in the independent sample.

The interpretation targets these specified artificial clonal haploid worlds, not natural biological stability. Original and limited food differ in both initial grass and regrowth, so those effects are not isolated. Estimate runtime from pilot and keep Totoro below 150 cores, with BLAS pinned to one thread. Archive source identity, all raw rows, pilot choice and source states.

## Revision after preliminary screen

The first screen had no passing candidate: threshold 120 and 100 left many assay bottlenecks; threshold 80 had only one bottleneck but exceeded the prespecified 1% cap limit and the all-block stability target. The source initializer also allowed overlapping founder cells. Retain this screen as diagnostic, and add a research-only source initialization with unique cells using a separate RNG, plus per-tick occupancy assertions in sources and assays. This changes the setup, so repeat the entire selection screen on independent seeds 50006–50010 before any final run. Test intermediate thresholds 95, 90, 85 in that order, using the same 96 assay and 100 source founders and the same gates. The first candidate that passes is carried forward. These values were selected to investigate the stability–capacity tradeoff, without using the mixture contrast. If none passes, do not label any as stable; revise further and record the change.

## Revision after unique-cell screen

No intermediate threshold passed all gates on seeds 50006–50010: 95 left 11 bottlenecked assays, 90 left five across three blocks, and 85 left one while exceeding the cap-time limit. To give populations more headroom, screen thresholds 95, 90 and 85 in that order with 144 assay founders, 150 source founders and a 700-agent cap on the same 900-cell grid. Each staggered age 25–72 appears three times. Use separate seeds 50011–50015, retaining all the earlier gates, with cap time now measured at 700. Track occupancy every tick and do not interpret the resulting higher-density world as equivalent to the earlier one. If no setting passes, report the obstruction or make an explicitly recorded further design revision.

Before the final campaign, verify the first passing large-population candidate on a further five independent blocks (50016–50020), using the same stability gates and additionally requiring less than 1% of source tick observations at the population cap. The source cap diagnostic was not recorded in the earlier screens, so the validation driver records it explicitly. These validation seeds are separate from both candidate selection and the final 51001–51100 blocks.

## Revision after larger-population screen

All three 144-founder candidates failed the stability gates, with more bottlenecks than the 96-founder screen. Return to 96 assay founders and 100 source founders, preserve distinct source cells and every-tick occupancy checks, and increase the hard cap to 700 on the 900-cell grid. Screen thresholds 85, 80 and 75, in that order, on fresh seeds 50021–50025. Count source and assay cap ticks separately and apply the same gates, including below 1% at the cap in each pooled source and assay set. Initial food and regrowth remain unchanged. Additional density itself may affect behaviour, so record peak population and report the spatial setting explicitly. If a candidate passes, check it on independent seeds 50026–50030 before the final seed set.

For the headroom screen, also require every source and assay endpoint to be classified `viable` by the package's existing endpoint rule: at least 50 of 100 source founders and at least 48 of 96 assay founders remain. This additional gate is set before inspecting the headroom-screen outcomes. The every-tick minimum threshold remains 20 and the final analysis retains all outcomes.

## Revision after independent validation

The threshold-80 validation on seeds 50026–50030 met the minimum and cap gates in all 180 assays, but two assays ended at 47 individuals, just below the package's endpoint-viable cutoff of 48; it therefore failed the predeclared requirement that every endpoint be viable. To avoid treating a one-individual boundary crossing as a universal failure while still requiring a high viability rate, revise the assay endpoint gate to at least 95% viable endpoints; retain 100% source endpoint viability, at least 95% of assays without a sub-20 dip, at least 90% fully stable blocks, the cap limits, and all matching and behaviour checks. This change follows the observed pilot and cannot retrospectively make it a strict pass. Validate the revised gate on fresh seeds 50031–50035 before running the independent final seed set. Retain weak and crashed endpoints in all analyses.
