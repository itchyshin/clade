# Matched founder-density and reproduction factorial

Test why the earlier food-dependent policy-mixture pattern differed between worlds. In each seed block, train one source population using the lower-bottleneck setup: 100 founders in distinct cells, reproductive threshold 80, haploid mutation SD .20, 700-agent cap. Select one panel of eight endpoint policies and use it in every assay cell. Source training is held fixed so the study isolates differences in assay setup, not the origin of the policies. An unavailable source is recorded and blocks that source's planned assays; never replace or silently discard it.

Cross four assay factors: founder count 48 versus 96; reproductive threshold 120 versus 80; original food (initial grass .50 and regrowth .05) versus moderate limitation (.25 and .025); and fixed founder age 50 versus staggered ages 25–72, repeated for 96 founders. In every cell, run one equal-policy mixture and eight 48- or 96-individual single-policy controls, for 144 simulations per block. Keep the cap at 700, unique starting cells and mutation disabled in all assays. Within each seed and cell, physical initial states match across the nine groups. Different founder counts use different random allocations; the source panel and treatment-independent seeding remain matched across cells.

For each block define D = mixture population minus the equal mean of the eight control populations. At tick 1,000 the planned food contrast F = D(limited) - D(original) is calculated for each founder count, reproductive threshold and age schedule. The main diagnostic contrasts are how F changes with threshold at fixed founder count and how F changes with founder count at fixed threshold, separately by age; the density-by-threshold interaction in F is also reported. Tick-800 contrasts and changes to tick 1,000 are secondary. Use paired-seed means and 95% t and bootstrap intervals, report all contrasts and treat intervals as unadjusted for multiple comparisons. No single non-significant contrast establishes equality. Source and assay bottlenecks, endpoint viability, extinction and cap contact are separate outcomes and never determine inclusion.

Pilot seed blocks 60001–60003, then if source generation, panel behaviour, matching, population accounting, unique occupancy and raw-data reconstruction pass, run 100 independent blocks 61001–61100. The pilot is excluded from the final analysis. The 100-block size matches earlier work and does not guarantee power. Screening does not require all cells to remain above 20: the original-threshold cells are expected to exhibit bottlenecks, which are part of the factorial comparison. Source states, failed blocks and all runs must be retained. Estimate runtime from the pilot, cap at 100 cores with BLAS pinned to one thread, and record execution metadata.

The study compares artificial haploid clonal populations in these exact worlds. Food treatments change initial grass and regrowth together; one-agent-per-cell is checked during the run. Founder count alters initial density and subsequent dynamics, while threshold may directly alter reproduction and feeding. Results cannot attribute all differences to bottlenecks or rule out other causal factors such as the source-population setup or the changed cap.
