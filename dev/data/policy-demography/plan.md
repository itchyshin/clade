# Births and deaths after matched policy switches

Replay the preceding 100 matched seed blocks and three arms with a passive observer. For every 50-tick interval, record births by the parent's age, deaths by the dying individual's age, and age-specific pre-mortality agent-ticks in bands 0–49, 50–99, 100–149 and 150+. Birth age is the parent's age after the tick's age increment, and death age is the individual's age at the same point. Verify that per-tick births and deaths equal the kernel counters and that births minus deaths reproduce each population change. Check all population, policy, age, grass and intervention records against the earlier run.

The main descriptive decomposition is the paired treatment-minus-sham change in cumulative births and deaths between ticks 600 and 800, and its 50-tick subdivisions. Report age contributions and agent-tick denominators as exploratory, with paired 95% intervals; do not infer individual mortality causes or mediation from a net accounting identity. Continue through tick 1,000 and retain all runs. Earlier comparisons identify this window, so the demographic analysis is follow-up rather than a preregistered independent test. Track extinction and endpoint viability. The intervention remains artificial, with density and age held equal only at tick 600.
