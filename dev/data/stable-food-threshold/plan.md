# Food-by-threshold interaction with reproductive timing

## Design and selection

Test the previously observed food-by-reproductive-threshold interaction in a population setting with few transient bottlenecks, and record reproductive timing. Use the same source panel and initial physical state across matched assay treatments apart from the threshold trait itself, which is encoded in the genome. Verify a normalized fingerprint that removes only that trait. Source training remains the earlier 100-founder, threshold-80, max-age-200, haploid, mutation-.2 world with unique cells. In assays, cross thresholds 80 and 120, original food (initial grass .50, regrowth .05) and limited food (.25, .025), fixed founder age 50 and staggered founder ages 25–72, and one eight-policy mixture with eight equal single-policy controls. Each assay has 96 founders, 700 cap, 900 cells and zero mutation. Both food components change together. Every candidate applies the same maximum age in every assay arm; changing lifespan defines a new world, so differences from earlier studies cannot be attributed to stability alone.

Earlier screens found frequent sub-20 dips at threshold 120. Screen assay maximum ages 250, 300 and 400, in that fixed order, using five source seeds 84001–84005, 72 assays per candidate and seed. Choose the first candidate meeting all conditions within each threshold separately: at least 95% assay endpoints viable under the package rule, at least 95% of assay runs never below 20 agents, fewer than 1% of all tick observations at the cap; source endpoint viable and no source history below 20; all source panels have eight distinct policies with behavioural disagreement; exact physical matching within threshold and normalized matching across thresholds, occupancy and population ledgers pass. Also report results separately by food and age and do not select based on estimated effects. This is an operational minimum and does not establish genetic stability. Failed candidate results are retained. If none passes, explicitly record that result before a separate reasoned revision, then re-screen; do not silently describe an unstable world as stable.

A first passing setting must pass the same checks on five fresh validation sources 84011–84015. Pilot and validation seeds are excluded from the main run. If validation fails, report the failure and revise before making any inference in the requested stable setting. After validation, run 100 disjoint source blocks 85001–85100, all 72 assays per source, with all completed runs retained regardless of bottlenecks or endpoint viability.

## Estimands

For each seed, food, threshold and founder-age world define D as the mixture population minus the mean of the eight monocultures at tick 1,000. Define F(threshold) = D(limited food) - D(original food), and T = F(80) - F(120), separately for same-age and staggered founders. Report mean paired-seed T and unadjusted 95% t intervals. Also report tick 800 and change from tick 800 to 1,000 as secondary, together with the food contrasts and each D. Multiple comparisons and the two age schedules are not adjusted; do not equate an interval spanning zero with equivalence.

For each run, count all births by parent age: under 50, 50–99, 100–149 and 150 or older, and follow each parent's first energy-, age- and cap-eligible opportunity before age 50 to their first subsequent birth, death or end of the run. Report the age-specific birth distributions and the 50-tick cumulative incidence of birth from the first eligible opportunity with a competing-death Aalen-Johansen calculation, accounting for administrative censoring; check the sensitivity among entrants with a full 50 ticks remaining. Distinguish a change in the age at which an individual becomes eligible from waiting time after eligibility. Cohort entrants are post-treatment and can differ between worlds, so timing contrasts are descriptive and do not establish mediation. Where a treatment has no young eligible entrants, report the outcome as undefined and keep the run in population analyses. Source blocks, not agents, are independent units. Retain every population outcome, including any extinct or failed runs, and report failed sources without replacement.

Validate an independent Python reconstruction of pilot individual cohorts from opportunity rows, population and age-birth ledgers, source policy hashes, matching, no-op replay at threshold 80/max-age 200, and competing-risk calculations against `survival::survfit`. Record hard-cap occupancy, population minima, endpoint viability and raw checksums. Do not claim that the measured interaction is caused by reproduction timing.

## Compute

Totoro only, at most 24 workers with four Julia threads each and BLAS one. Estimate the 15-seed candidate screen at 4–8 minutes from the previous 900-assay pilot taking 124 seconds; stop at 8 minutes and re-estimate. Independent validation should take at most 5 minutes. After a passing validation, estimate a 100-source main run from the validated pilot; expected order 10–25 minutes, with a firm deadline and progress meter. If the estimate exceeds three hours, get approval before the full run.
