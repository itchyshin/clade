# Independent food-by-founder-age replication

Before inspecting the pilot, fix the new source seed blocks at 40001–40100, disjoint from the previous source campaigns 10001–10100 and 20001–20100. Each block generates its own viable haploid source population with founder mutation SD .05 and evolutionary mutation SD .20, using the earlier 1,000-tick procedure. Select the same eight endpoint policies for all four assay worlds within a block. The new assay disables mutation and uses 48 equal-density founders. Cross original food (initial grass .50, regrowth .05) versus moderate limitation (.25, .025) with fixed founder age 50 versus staggered ages 25–72. Compare one eight-policy mixture with the equal mean of eight single-policy populations per cell, for 36 runs per block.

Primary outcomes are the mixture-minus-control population difference at tick 800, and its paired change from tick 800 to 1,000, estimated separately for each of the four worlds. The food and age interaction contrasts are planned secondary analyses. Use seed blocks as independent units, 95% paired t intervals and bootstrap sensitivity. These intervals are descriptive for four conditions and two endpoints, without multiplicity adjustment; do not interpret them as simultaneous confirmatory tests. Include every run, even when nonviable, and report source and assay viability separately. Failures of source generation or fewer than eight available endpoint brains are recorded and block interpretation; no replacement seeds or selection of viable runs.

Pilot the first five blocks before continuing. Required checks: all source runs finish, have at least eight policies and are viable at the endpoint; selected policy means are distinct and show some deterministic behavioural disagreement across the common 48 contexts; all 180 assay runs complete with exact 50-tick birth/death ledgers and matched starting physical hashes for the nine arms in each cell; independent re-analysis reproduces the means; and no systematic collapse invalidates the viability premise. Record the full distribution and define viability by `viability_report()`. The viability check is diagnostic, not an exclusion criterion. Pilot blocks are retained in the 100-block result; any change to the design after inspecting their outcomes must be documented and the pilot considered exploratory.

Before launch estimate time from the measured pilot. Use at most eight Totoro workers with four Julia threads and one BLAS thread each. Archive source commit, modified assay patch, source and panel identities, every run and 50-tick window, execution receipts and failure logs. These 100 blocks match the previous replication count rather than guaranteeing power; interpretation depends on observed uncertainty. Fixed and staggered ages differ in mean by 1.5 ticks and the food regimes jointly change initial grass and regrowth.
