# Age-specific reproduction interventions

The preceding exploratory replay suggests that the food-related mixture contrast shifts between young and old parents when the reproductive threshold changes. To test whether access to reproduction at those ages affects that population pattern, intervene on reproduction in a 2-by-2 factorial: young gate off/on (parents under 50) and old gate off/on (parents 150 or older). In an active band, independently keyed deterministic pseudorandom assignment blocks approximately half of eligible reproductive opportunities. The key uses source seed, individual ID and tick, consumes no simulation RNG, and is shared across matched arms. The sham invokes the same observer but blocks nothing. The mechanism acts after age, energy and cap eligibility and before mate, local-space and reproductive costs; blocked opportunities therefore retain their potential energy cost. Other ages and opportunities remain unchanged.

Each new source block generates one haploid policy panel with the previously validated 100-founder, threshold-80 source setup. Each panel supplies matched 48/96-founder, threshold-120/80, original/limited-food, same/staggered-age worlds with one mixture and eight equal-density monocultures. Cross these 16 worlds with the four gating conditions, yielding 576 assay runs per block. Initial density, ages, positions, scalar traits, food and simulation random state match across gates and policy treatments within each world. They can change after interventions. Assay mutation is disabled. The source evolution is never gated. Reuse the same source panel across every cell and include all outcomes, including extinct populations and failed viability checks.

For seed and world define D = mixture population minus the mean of eight monoculture populations at tick 1,000, and F = D(limited food) - D(original food). At each gate and founder count, let T = F(threshold 80) - F(threshold 120). The main diagnostic is how T at 48 founders changes under each young or old single gate, relative to sham, with fixed and staggered founder ages reported separately. The two-gate interaction, 96-founder results, and tick-800 and time-change outcomes are secondary. Prespecify paired seed-level means, 95% t intervals and bootstrap sensitivity. Multiple comparisons are not adjusted. No absence of evidence establishes equivalence. This is an intervention on age-specific opportunities in an evolving population, not a natural mediation analysis; prevented parents retain energy, and gate effects may work through age, density and policy composition.

Record eligible opportunities, blocked counts and births by parent-age band, plus populations, exposure, grass intake and deaths every 50 ticks. A gate check must show no blocks in sham, no blocks outside targeted age bands, approximately half of targeted eligible opportunities blocked, and 50-tick event conservation. Check initial physical matching and distinct policy behaviours. A pilot uses one new block 70001, split across four gate workers. A separate prior-source sham replay on seed 61001 checks that the no-op kernel hook reproduces every earlier population, food and demographic window. Pilot outcomes cannot select a gate or seed. Before any full run audit all data, compare source panel hashes across pilot gate workers, and inspect viability/extinctions without discarding them. If instrumentation or accounting fails, fix and repeat; if intervention-induced collapse makes interpretation impossible, report the issue and revise the intervention explicitly.

After a successful pilot, run 100 seed-disjoint blocks 71001–71100 with all gate modes and retain every planned outcome. Source failures with fewer than eight policies are recorded, not replaced. The seed-block count matches earlier experiments and does not guarantee power. Estimate Totoro time from pilot, use at most 100 cores with BLAS pinned to one thread, and preserve source hashes, code, logs and raw outcomes. The design tests the specified halving intervention and the exact artificial haploid clonal worlds; it does not establish general claims about natural age-dependent reproduction or isolate initial grass from regrowth.

Before inspecting the pilot, require at least 95% of sham assay endpoints and at least 80% in each active gate to be classified viable; these checks are pilot diagnostics rather than a basis for excluding runs. A treatment with widespread collapse needs redesign and a fresh pilot. Targeted blocking fractions must fall between 45% and 55% of recorded eligible opportunities when aggregated over the pilot; untargeted blocks must be zero. All four pilot gate arms share one source seed, so these checks do not establish general stability.
